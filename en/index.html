Client work

Two business owners, two trades. They do what they love; their app handles the rest.

You run a bookshop because you love books.We handle the rest.

Librairie Matulu
Independent bookshop

The need
Every day, staff reviewed customer orders, book deliveries and inventory by hand. Distributor promotions arrived by email, PDF and Word, each in its own format.
What was built
  • Sort a delivery: finds the books a customer is waiting for, and shows which order each book belongs to.
  • Check customer orders: every morning, generates the list of books to pull from the shelves, missing copies and orders ready to go.
  • Promotions to purchase list: reads offers from four distributors, compares them with inventory and suggests what to order, by priority.
The result
Reports become Excel files that open with the actions to take. Besides saving time, the app reduces errors. The bookseller can take the time to share a love of books with the community.

You’re the video expert.We handle the rest.

Dakshan Visuals
Video production and editing

The need
Every batch of footage from an agency, several dozen clips, took hours of preparation before editing could even start: unzipping, filing, separating shots, building timelines, then fixing subtitles and exporting each video.
What was built
  • Prepare a batch: from the received zip file to a project ready to edit, with folders created, A and B shots separated by speech detection and silences trimmed.
  • Fix subtitles: compares the subtitles generated automatically by the editing software with subtitles produced by a local AI on the editor’s computer, for greater reliability.
  • Export the videos: finds each video on the edited timeline and exports it under the client’s naming convention.
The result
The editor opens a timeline that is already prepared and can let their creativity run free to offer clients more.

How it works

  1. A first meeting

    You describe the task that eats your time: what you do, how often, and what happens when a mistake slips in. A visit can be arranged for an outside perspective.

  2. A look at your real files

    The work starts from your needs. Your workflows are documented, and we confirm with you what will be built before any code is written.

  3. A verified automation

    Results are cross-checked by hand against your files. Every rule applied is visible in the output, so you can trust it or challenge it.

  4. Delivery

    You receive the app under your name, ready to install on your computers, with support contact details shown inside.
