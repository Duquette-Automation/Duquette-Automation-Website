Client work

Two businesses, two trades. They do what they love; their app handles the rest.

You run a bookshop because you love books.We handle the rest.

Librairie Matulu
Independent bookshop

The need
Every day, staff cross-checked customer orders, book deliveries and the inventory in their Vilivre software by hand. Distributor promotions arrived on top of that by email, PDF and Word, each in its own format.
What was built
  • Sort a delivery: finds books shelved for sale while a customer is waiting for them, and shows which order each received book belongs to.
  • Check customer orders: every morning, the books to pull from the shelves, missing copies and orders ready to go.
  • Promotions to purchase list: reads offers from four distributors, compares them with inventory and suggests what to order, by priority.
The result
Vilivre’s reports become Excel files that open with the actions to take. The app also keeps count of the time saved and the errors it has caught.

You’re the video expert.We handle the rest.

Dakshan Visuals
Video production and editing

The need
Every batch of footage from an agency, dozens of clips for nine videos, took hours of preparation before editing could even start: unzipping, filing, separating shots, building timelines, then fixing subtitles and exporting each video.
What was built
  • Prepare a batch: from the received zip file to a DaVinci Resolve project ready to edit: folders created, A and B shots separated by speech detection, silences trimmed.
  • Fix subtitles: compares generated subtitles with the script and a glossary of names and regional words, then places a marker on every doubtful passage.
  • Export the videos: finds each video on the edited timeline and exports it under the client’s naming convention, never overwriting a version.
The result
The editor opens a timeline that is already prepared and only reviews the flagged subtitles. Everything runs on the editing workstation: no subscription, no footage sent anywhere.

How it works

  1. A first call

    You describe the task that eats your time: what you do, how often, and what happens when a mistake slips in.

  2. A look at your real files

    The work starts from your actual exports. Hidden rules, like exceptions or misleading columns, are identified and confirmed with you before any code is written.

  3. A verified automation

    Results are cross-checked by hand against your files. Every rule applied is visible in the output, so you can trust it or challenge it.

  4. Delivery

    You receive the app under your name, ready to install on your computers, with support contact details shown inside.
